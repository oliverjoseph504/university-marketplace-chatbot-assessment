
# Common User Pain Points in Student Marketplaces

1. Unreliable communication: Buyers frequently message sellers but never follow through, and sellers may respond slowly or stop replying, leading to wasted time and uncertainty.
2. Incomplete or unclear listings: Many posts lack key information such as item condition, available stock, pickup location, or clear photos, making it difficult for buyers to make decisions.
3. Scheduling difficulties: Coordinating meetup times around busy student schedules often leads to cancellations or missed pickups.

# Safety and Trust Challenges in Campus Communities

1. Risk of scams or fake listings: Some sellers advertise items that do not exist or request payment before meeting.
2. Sale of restricted items such as alcohol
3. Unsafe meetup situations: Students may feel uncomfortable meeting strangers off-campus or in isolated areas.
4. Misrepresentation of item condition: Items described as “like new” may actually be damaged or heavily used.

# Seasonal Patterns in Student Marketplaces

1. Start-of-semester demand spike: Students heavily search for textbooks, calculators, electronics, and dorm essentials.
2. Move-out period selling surge: Students sell unneeded items  before leaving campus.

# Integration Needs with University Systems

1. University email verification: Restricting accounts to valid university emails helps ensure users are actual students.
2. Student identity verification: Linking accounts to student IDs increases accountability and trust.
3. Campus location integration: Recommending safe meetup locations on campus (libraries, student centers, etc.) can improve transaction safety.
